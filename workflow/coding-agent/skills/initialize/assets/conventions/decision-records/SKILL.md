# decision-records

**This skill is a stub.** The workflow expects a `decision-records` convention at this point, and what it found here is an uninitialized placeholder.

A decision-records convention states how this project records a settled decision: where the record lives, what it carries, and how a later decision supersedes an earlier one. Until the project states it, the workflow has no durable decision to cite.

These questions may help establish it. Take them or leave them:

- Where do settled decisions live?
- What does a decision record carry - the decision, its rationale, the alternatives it rejected?
- Does a record cite its own reasoning, or may it lean on a record that might be pruned?
- How does a later decision supersede an earlier one?
