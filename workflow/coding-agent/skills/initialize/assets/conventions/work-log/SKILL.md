# work-log

**This skill is a stub.** The workflow expects a `work-log` convention at this point, and what it found here is an uninitialized placeholder.

A work-log convention states how this project records completed work and open exploration. It covers two structures, each with its own asset beside this file:

- `assets/handover.md` - the work-unit record: what a finished unit of work leaves behind.
- `assets/discussion.md` - the exploration record: a design question examined before it settles.

Until the project states them, the workflow has no work log to write or read.

These questions may help establish it. Take them or leave them:

- Where does a work unit's record live, and how is it identified - a date and a sequence, a plain sequence, or nothing?
- What sections does a work-unit record carry, and which are mandatory?
- Where does an exploration record live, and when does it become a settled decision instead?
- Are old records kept in the working tree, or rotated out after they are distilled?
