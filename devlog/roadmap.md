## Upcoming Milestones

### M3 - Manual Dispatch, Autonomous Execution, Manual Review

**Objective:** Move from interactive prompting to structured single-task execution with enough logging to verify the agent is doing useful work. Requires the two-layer foundation from M2.

**Depends on:** the M2 two-layer foundation, closed in [`changelog.md`](changelog.md#m2---reasoningcapability-layer-separation) (headless mode requires the capability layer tool interface; task briefs are the operator input channel from M1.5).

**Finding -- Sub-milestone containment (recorded, not designed):** Milestones and sub-milestones are intended to be self-contained, but partial implementations from later milestones are frequently needed while the current milestone is incomplete. This suggests that how features are cut into sub-milestones, and how strictly they are sequenced, may be the wrong seam. The sub-milestone-as-container model is recognized as a candidate for re-examination, not as settled. Design and any restructuring is deferred to M3. The current deferred-items / sub-milestone task-list system is maintained until then.

#### M3.1 - Backpressure

**Objective:** every commit gets gate feedback, cheaply enough that the author waits for it rather than reaching for the bypass. M3.1.1 holds the mechanisms that landed; M3.1.2 holds the gates' own upkeep, which is open because adding a gate and keeping it fast are both unowned.

#### M3.1.2 - Gate upkeep

The copy-delivery Markdown `pre-commit` hook is the first instance ([`git_hooks.md`](../docs/adr/git_hooks.md)); mount delivery carries no hook under the current delivery constraint. Two things about the gates are unowned: what they cost the author, and the procedure for changing one. The gates themselves are not in question here.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- A commit's gate cost is measured on a realistic staged change and stated in [`bash-coding-conventions.md`](../docs/development/bash-coding-conventions.md), so a regression has a number to be measured against.
- Adding, amending and retiring a gate is a documented procedure a new maintainer can follow without asking, and a gate that is enabled but not registered fails loudly rather than silently doing nothing.

- [ ] **Shorten the wall-clock cost of the gates** -- measure the hook on a realistic staged change and the lint umbrella over the tree, then take the largest share down. The 2026-09 study measured the umbrella at about 30 seconds before the parallel-shellcheck change and it is about 5 seconds now; nothing states the budget, so nothing says when it has regressed. The T2 telemetry rows are the measurement source for the subagent and tool-call costs that compete with gate cost in the author's wait.
  - [ ] **State the budget and where it lives** -- a number in the conventions doc, and a row that fails when the measurement exceeds it.
- [ ] **Formalize the procedure for adding, amending or removing a gate** -- the draft braindump is [`precommit-gate-runbook.md`](../workflow/coding-agent/drafts/precommit-gate-runbook.md), carrying the proposals from the 2026-10-02 session review and the measured markdownlint-cli2 mechanics. Refinement happens here. Raised by the record-correction prompt (handover `20261002-14`).
  - [ ] **The exempted record trees hold 1,195 dead links** -- `devlog/handovers/`, `devlog/discussions/` and `docs/adr/archive/` carry relative links that point at files which do not exist, mostly paths that moved when `devlog` was restructured at M1.5. The gate prints the exempt file count every run and does not measure this. Re-path them or record an accepted baseline, and give the tree list an owner and an end the way `legacyFiles` has one.

#### T10 - Argument, configuration and operator surface

The surface through which a setting or a command enters the harness, and the surface through which the harness answers the operator. Five design problems from the read-through share it, and [`docs/adr/env_resolution.md`](../docs/adr/env_resolution.md) already fixes the requirements the first row serves.

- [ ] **Resolution rule: one ladder for every setting** -- nine readers over four record shapes re-implement one ladder, `env_resolve_one`'s explicit branch is unreachable, and its failure text names the identity setting inside a generic function. Evidence rows 22, 38, 72, 78. Source: [`20260924-design-draft-resolver_contract.md`](../devlog/discussions/20260924-design-draft-resolver_contract.md), the resolution half of Note A.
- [ ] **Environment: one load path, no repaired damage** -- `.env` is a config source and a runtime environment at once, `env_load` mis-parses two common shapes silently, and the prelude re-asserts the identity triple as tax on the bulk load. Evidence rows 47, 48, 180.
- [ ] **CLI-to-leaf contract: the router and its handoff** -- the subcommand set appears in four lists that already disagree, identity is resolved four different ways and forwarded in four different sets, and the `exec` handoff carries a help status no caller maps. Evidence rows 48, 130, 136, 173. Source: [`20260924-design-draft-cli_workflow_interface.md`](../devlog/discussions/20260924-design-draft-cli_workflow_interface.md). **Also in scope: the `_CLI_TOLERANT` cleanup (read-through row 137).** `cli.sh` keeps a tolerant parse mode (`_CLI_TOLERANT=1` drops unknown flags) that only `scripts/prune.sh` still uses, a migration holdover from the shared-parser adoption. A destructive command should not drop unknown flags: remove the knob from `cli.sh`, make prune's parse strict (a one-line unset), and retire the leniency requirement from ADR `command_flag_parsing.md`. **Clause landed (2026-09-27):** the `_CLI_TOLERANT` knob is removed, prune parses strictly and rejects an unknown flag before pruning anything, the ADR records the removal, and strict-mode coverage replaces the two tolerant-mode tests. Ported from the parallel-track trial, unit U1. The rest of the row -- the four disagreeing subcommand lists, the four identity resolutions and the `exec` handoff's unmapped help status -- is untouched.
- [ ] **Makefile-to-CLI contract: one invocation idiom** -- sixteen `$(if)` translators for one boolean convention, `PROVIDER=pi` beside `--provider=pi`, and a second help and error surface that must match the CLI. Evidence rows 44, 45, 46. T4 owns the separate question of whether make stays.
- [ ] **Interactive front end: the helper hands back a command** -- the model is that the helper asks the unanswered questions and returns a fully specified command the leaf executes; today the tree returns one token or confirms a plan the leaf already built, so the printed command does not drive the run. Evidence rows 148, 166, 188, 264, 265. Source: [`20260925-design-draft-interactive_command_contract.md`](../devlog/discussions/20260925-design-draft-interactive_command_contract.md).
- [ ] **The operator-facing surface: hints, help, usage and the session display** -- the hints are defined per surface rather than once, `resume` always continues into standard mode with no document saying so, and the session display's staleness marker breaks its own row alignment. Design: [`20260925-design-draft-operator_surface.md`](../devlog/discussions/20260925-design-draft-operator_surface.md), draft. Evidence rows 37, 81, 198; row 290's lint-gate ADR rides the M3.1 gate rows.
- [ ] **Note A: configuration, state and argument surface** -- the sector note and the policy doc the rows above satisfy. Its declaration and transport half (rows 41 to 48) has no note yet, and the Makefile arm of row 48 stays with the parser doc.

#### T11 - Persisted record formats

The shapes the harness writes and reads back: session state, the draft state, the session log, the dry-run record and the runner's report. One record family, one reader and writer pair each, and structured where structured pays.

- [ ] **Record shape: one KV read/write pair** -- a `kv_read` with a `kv_write`, plus a multi-key load, behind every key-value record: the `.draft-state` reader and writer have no single definition and five of the writer's eight fields are unpinned, the session log's read contradicts its own docstring, and the state readers overlap. The `.draft-state` eval the immediate lane closed is the class this contract removes by construction. Evidence rows 17, 22, 24, 25, 39, 40, 50, 105, 303.
- [ ] **Structured where it helps: JSON and the parser dependency** -- which records become structured, and the dependency that costs: JSON is the easiest reach, given a willingness to take a parser rather than roll one. Row 20.
- [ ] **One timestamp format** -- `YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS` has no definition: ten producers, three runtime readers, and three test files pinning the literal. Row 31.
- [ ] **One relative-time ladder** -- two densities, the plural rule copied four times, and two epoch round-trips. Row 32.
