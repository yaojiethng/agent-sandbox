# Evolve an ADR in place

Open this when a principle changes and the live ADR must carry the new decision.

1. Move the current entry's content to the historical position.
2. Add the new decision as the new `Current:` entry, in the template's field order.
3. On the demoted entry, add a line saying why the old pattern was rejected for the new one.
4. Condense the demoted entry where possible, especially where it applied the pattern rather than changed it. Drop the mechanical detail no reader needs. Keep the decision and its reason, and do not delete or rewrite its rationale.
