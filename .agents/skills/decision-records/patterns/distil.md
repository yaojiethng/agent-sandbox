# Distil a design document into an ADR

Open this when a discussion or design document has settled a principle and the ADR does not exist yet.

1. Remove delivery-sequence framing. "Change N", "prerequisite" and "introduced in" describe the road, not the principle.
2. Remove command shapes and implementation detail. The reference document that describes the implementation owns those.
3. Keep the primitives, the invariants, the design rationale, and any collision or interaction table.
4. Keep links to the design and discussion documents while the change is in development.

What remains is the current entry. The rejected alternatives are the ones the design considered and dropped, each with its reason.
